How SahYog Works
Every reported problem moves through the same six stages, from first report to citizen-verified resolution.
1

Report

Citizen submits a problem with description, location and evidence.

2

AI Classify

Rule-based analysis identifies category, severity and affected area.

3

Smart Match

Platform recommends suitable departments, universities, industries and NGOs.

4

Collaborate

Problem solvers review the challenge and work together on a solution.

5

Deploy

The approved solution moves toward on-ground implementation.

6

Verify

Citizens confirm whether the problem was actually resolved.

What happens after you submit

Our classifier reads your title and description to suggest a category, subcategory and the most relevant authority. This runs on keyword rules for the demo — clearly marked AI Analysis — Prototype — and the code is structured so a trained NLP model can be swapped in.

Evidence review. Uploaded photos are stored against your Problem ID for the assigned organization to review.

Your privacy. Your exact location is visible to the assigned organization only; the public Explore feed shows just the general area.
